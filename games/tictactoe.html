About Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a classic strategy game that is easy to learn and fun to play. In this free online version, you play as X against the computer, which plays as O.

Choose Easy Mode for a relaxed experience or Hard Mode to challenge an intelligent computer opponent. Try to get three matching symbols in a row and win the game!

How to Play Tic Tac Toe

  1. You play first as X.
  2. Tap any empty square on the 3×3 game board.
  3. The computer will make its move as O.
  4. Take turns until someone gets three matching symbols in a row.
  5. Choose Restart to play another round.
  6. Use Reset Score to clear the scoreboard.

Tic Tac Toe Rules

  • The game is played on a 3×3 grid.
  • You are X and the computer is O.
  • Players take turns placing their symbols in empty squares.
  • The first player to make a line of three matching symbols wins.
  • A winning line can be horizontal, vertical or diagonal.
  • If all nine squares are filled and nobody wins, the game is a draw.
  • You cannot place a symbol in an already occupied square.

Tic Tac Toe Tips and Tricks

1. Take the Center

The center square is useful because it can be part of several possible winning lines.

2. Watch Your Opponent

Always check whether the computer is about to complete a line. Block its winning move when necessary.

3. Create Winning Opportunities

Try to create two possible winning lines at the same time. This can make it harder for your opponent to stop you.

4. Practice Hard Mode

Hard Mode uses a strategy-based computer opponent. Practice against it to improve your decision-making skills.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tic Tac Toe free to play?

Yes! You can play this Tic Tac Toe game online for free.

Can I play against the computer?

Yes. You play as X and the computer plays as O.

What is the difference between Easy and Hard Mode?

Easy Mode makes random moves. Hard Mode uses the Minimax strategy to choose its moves.

How do I win the game?

Make a line of three X symbols horizontally, vertically or diagonally before the computer does.

Can a game end in a draw?

Yes. If all nine squares are filled and neither player has three symbols in a row, the game ends in a draw.

Does the game work on mobile phones?

Yes. The game is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices.
