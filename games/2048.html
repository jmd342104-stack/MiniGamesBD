About 2048 Game

2048 is a fun and addictive number puzzle game that challenges your logical thinking and planning skills. The goal is to slide numbered tiles around a 4×4 board and combine matching numbers until you create the 2048 tile.

Every move adds a new tile to the board, so you need to plan carefully. Play for free on Mini Games BD, challenge yourself and try to beat your highest score!

How to Play 2048

  1. Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to move the tiles.
  2. On mobile, swipe up, down, left or right on the game board.
  3. Tiles with the same number combine when they touch.
  4. Each successful combination creates a tile with double the value.
  5. Keep combining tiles until you reach 2048.
  6. Use the Undo button to reverse your previous move.

Game Rules

  • The game board contains 16 spaces arranged in a 4×4 grid.
  • New tiles appear with a value of 2 or 4 after a valid move.
  • Only tiles with the same number can merge.
  • Two matching tiles merge into one tile with their combined value.
  • Each tile can merge only once during a single move.
  • The game ends when no empty spaces or possible merges remain.
  • Reach the 2048 tile to achieve the main winning goal.

Tips and Tricks to Win

1. Keep Your Biggest Tile in One Corner

Try to keep your highest-value tile in a single corner. This makes organizing the other numbers easier.

2. Avoid Random Moves

Think before moving. Random swipes can scatter your tiles and make it difficult to combine larger numbers.

3. Build Numbers Gradually

Combine smaller tiles step by step. Focus on creating a clear path toward larger numbers.

4. Keep Empty Spaces Available

Empty spaces give you more options. Try not to fill the entire board too quickly.

5. Use Undo Carefully

If your last move creates a difficult situation, use Undo to return to the previous board and try another direction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 2048 free to play?

Yes. You can play 2048 online for free on Mini Games BD.

How do I move the tiles?

Use the arrow keys on a computer or swipe in any direction on your mobile device.

What happens when two matching tiles meet?

They merge into one tile with double their original value. For example, two 8 tiles create a 16 tile.

Can I undo a move?

Yes. The Undo button lets you reverse your most recent valid move. You can undo one move at a time.

Does the game save my best score?

Yes. Your best score is saved locally in your browser using local storage.

When does the game end?

The game ends when the board is full and there are no adjacent matching tiles that can merge.
