Welcome to Mini Games BD. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you visit and use our website.

Mini Games BD does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information to play games. We do not intentionally collect personal information through our games.

Some games may use your browser's local storage to save your best scores or game progress. This information stays on your device and is not sent to our server by these games. You can clear this data through your browser settings.

We may display advertisements in the future to support the website. Third-party advertising providers, such as Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure advertisements. Their data practices will be governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy policies, content, or practices of those websites. Please read their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

6. Contact Form

Our contact form may open your device's email application to let you send us a message. Any information you include in that email will be handled by your email provider and the recipient of the message.

7. Children's Privacy

Mini Games BD does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the concern.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep our website safe. However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
