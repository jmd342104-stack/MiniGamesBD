About Whack-a-Mole

Whack-a-Mole is a fun and fast-paced arcade reflex game. Moles appear randomly in different holes, and your job is to tap them before they disappear. Every successful hit earns you one point.

You have only 30 seconds to catch as many moles as possible. Challenge your reaction speed, improve your focus and try to beat your personal best score. Play for free on Mini Games BD!

How to Play Whack-a-Mole

  1. Press the Start Game button to begin.
  2. Watch the nine holes on the game board.
  3. Tap or click the hole where the mole appears.
  4. Each successful hit gives you one point.
  5. Keep catching moles before the 30-second timer ends.
  6. Press Restart whenever you want to start over.

Game Rules

  • Each round lasts 30 seconds.
  • There are nine holes where the mole can appear.
  • Only tapping the hole with the active mole earns a point.
  • Each mole can be hit only once.
  • Moles appear at random positions.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.
  • The game ends automatically when the timer reaches zero.

Tips to Get a Higher Score

1. Stay Focused

Keep your eyes on the entire board instead of watching only one hole. The mole can appear in any of the nine positions.

2. React Quickly

Tap the mole as soon as you see it. Quick reactions help you collect more points before the timer runs out.

3. Use Your Fingers on Mobile

On a touchscreen device, use your thumb or index finger to tap the mole directly. Keep your hand comfortable.

4. Avoid Random Tapping

Only tap when you can see the mole. Random tapping will not increase your score.

5. Practice Regularly

Play multiple rounds to improve your reaction time and try to beat your previous best score.
