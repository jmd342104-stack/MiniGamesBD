About Snake Game

Snake Game is a classic arcade game where you control a growing snake and collect food to increase your score. The more food you eat, the longer your snake becomes.

This free online Snake Game is designed for both mobile phones and desktop computers. Use the arrow buttons or keyboard controls to guide your snake and try to beat your personal best score.

How to Play Snake Game

  1. Press the Start Game button to begin.
  2. Use the arrow buttons on your phone to control the snake.
  3. On a computer, use the Arrow Keys on your keyboard.
  4. Move the snake toward the red food.
  5. Each food item increases your score and makes the snake longer.
  6. Use Pause to stop the game temporarily.
  7. Press Restart to begin a new game.

Snake Game Rules

  • Collect food to increase your score.
  • Your snake grows longer after eating food.
  • Do not hit the walls of the game board.
  • Do not let the snake hit its own body.
  • The game ends when the snake hits a wall or itself.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser using local storage.

Snake Game Tips and Tricks

1. Plan Your Moves

Look ahead before changing direction. Avoid moving into corners where your snake may get trapped.

2. Stay Away from Your Body

As your snake grows, it becomes harder to move around. Always leave enough space for your next turn.

3. Avoid Sudden Turns

Make careful turns and think about where your snake will move next.

4. Practice Regularly

Play regularly to improve your reaction time and control. Try to beat your best score with every new game.
