About Memory Match Game

Memory Match is a fun and simple brain game that challenges your memory and concentration. Your goal is to find all matching emoji pairs hidden behind the cards.

This free online Memory Match game includes 16 cards with 8 matching pairs. Flip two cards at a time, remember their positions and try to match every pair using as few moves as possible.

You can play directly in your browser on a mobile phone, tablet or computer. No account or payment is required.

How to Play Memory Match

  1. Tap or click any card to reveal its emoji.
  2. Select a second card to find its matching pair.
  3. If both emojis match, the cards will remain visible.
  4. If they do not match, both cards will turn face down again.
  5. Remember the positions of previously revealed emojis.
  6. Continue until you match all 8 pairs.

How to Win

Match all 8 pairs of emojis to complete the game. Try to finish with fewer moves and a faster time.

Memory Match Game Rules

  • There are 16 cards in total.
  • Each emoji appears exactly twice.
  • You can reveal only two cards at a time.
  • Matching cards stay visible on the board.
  • Non-matching cards turn face down after a short delay.
  • Each completed pair counts as one successful match.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips and Tricks to Improve Your Memory

1. Remember Card Positions

Pay attention to the location of every emoji you reveal. Remembering positions makes it easier to find matching pairs.

2. Play Slowly and Carefully

Do not rush your moves. Take a moment to remember the emojis before selecting another card.

3. Focus on Unmatched Cards

Try to remember cards that have not been matched yet. This helps you avoid unnecessary moves.

4. Beat Your Best Score

Play again and try to complete the game using fewer moves than your previous attempt.
