About Rock Paper Scissors

Rock Paper Scissors is a popular hand game played around the world. It is simple, exciting and based on choosing one of three moves: Rock, Paper or Scissors.

In this free online version, you compete against the computer. Make your choice, see the computer's move and find out who wins each round.

How to Play Rock Paper Scissors

  1. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors by tapping one of the three buttons.
  2. The computer will randomly select its own move.
  3. Both choices will appear on the screen.
  4. The game will announce whether you win, lose or draw.
  5. Your scores will automatically update after every round.
  6. Press Reset Score whenever you want to start over.

Rock Paper Scissors Rules

  • Rock beats Scissors.
  • Scissors beats Paper.
  • Paper beats Rock.
  • If both players choose the same move, the round is a draw.
  • Every round is played independently.
  • The scoreboard tracks your wins, computer wins and draws.

Tips and Tricks

1. Understand the Rules

Remember which move defeats the others. Knowing the three basic rules is the first step to playing well.

2. Think Before Choosing

Take a moment to consider your choice instead of tapping randomly.

3. Enjoy Every Round

The computer makes a random choice, so every round can have a different result.

4. Track Your Score

Watch your wins and draws on the scoreboard and see how well you perform over multiple rounds.
